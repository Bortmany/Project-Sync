**Date:** 2026-09-01

## Executive summary

- Confirmed real (not roadmap) from code/docs: discipline-task model with auto-derived progress, built-in time-boxed cross-company access, Gantt charts, MS365 OneDrive/SharePoint attach, Paddle billing (dormant), flat no-per-seat plan model.

## 1. Shipped features confirmed (code/docs, not roadmap)

Multi-tenant self-signup with industry templates (oil & gas, construction, blank); projects → main tasks → discipline tasks (up to 8 disciplines: Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Civil, Process, HSE, Reliability, Inspection), with main-task progress **always derived**, never hand-typed (tested golden rule); **cross-company collaboration built into the architecture** — time-boxed, admin-approved external access with optional sign-off requirement (`externalTaskScope()`, `external-scoping.service.test.ts`), not a bolted-on guest hack; documents with permanent revision history + audit trail + **Microsoft 365 OneDrive/SharePoint attach** (one org-level Azure connection, `Files.Read.All` only, dormant until Azure app registered); **Gantt/timeline charts** (project, task, and my-tasks views — real, not placeholder); Admin billing live now (Free: 1 project/10 people/500MB; Pro: unlimited projects/people, 10GB) with Paddle payment collection built but dormant pending 4 env vars; Slack/Teams via pasted webhook (no OAuth app yet); 2FA, RBAC, rate limiting, CSP, GDPR-style self-service export/delete, full-text search. **No per-seat concept exists in the schema at all.**

## 3. Sentiment (repeated themes, with links)

- **Procore**: praised for unlimited-user value at 100+ users; repeatedly criticized for steep learning curve, hard org-wide rollout, and an opaque quote-only sales process ("the single biggest complaint in the Procore world right now") — directly maps to Tielora's transparent flat pricing. [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/56250/Procore/reviews/), [Procore pricing analysis](https://projul.com/blog/procore-pricing-analysis-2026/)
- **PlanRadar**: praised for mobile field workflow (4.3★ weighted); complaints on onboarding overload and mobile slowdown at scale. [G2 pros/cons](https://www.g2.com/products/planradar/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)
- **Fieldwire**: praised as simple/fast to learn with strong offline mode; complaints on clunky web-to-mobile project sync and no time tracking. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/142801/Fieldwire/reviews/)
- **monday.com**: notification overload by default is a repeated complaint; the 3-seat minimum is a real trial-friction point. [3-seat minimum](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/monday-pricing)
- **Asana** — most relevant to Tielora's positioning: any invited external guest can invite further guests with **zero admin control** unless paying for Enterprise (~$3K+ tier); onboarding external contractors is reported as "not straightforward." [Asana Forum complaint](https://forum.asana.com/t/external-guest-permissions/336067)
- **ClickUp**: "Learning Curve" is G2's 2nd most-cited complaint (1,752+ mentions); reliability issues at scale (lag, sync conflicts); AI add-on cost is billed per seat regardless of use. [G2 pros/cons](https://www.g2.com/products/clickup/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)
- **Cross-cutting theme**: none of the 8 tools researched offer a first-class, no-extra-seat, admin-scoped "another company works inside this one project only" model — every per-seat tool makes occasional outside participants either costly or under-governed. This is Tielora's clearest structural opening.

## 5. Pro pricing recommendation (current $29/mo placeholder)

**Keep it flat — do not switch to per-seat.** Per-seat billing is exactly what generates the guest/external-collaborator friction documented above; switching would undercut Tielora's own positioning.

**But $29/mo flat is priced far below the market** — Fieldwire's cheapest paid tier alone is $39/user/mo (≈$390/mo for a 10-person team, 13x Tielora's placeholder); PlanRadar starts at $49/user/mo; even a 10-seat Asana Advanced runs ~$250/mo and 10-seat monday Pro ~$190–240/mo. Underpricing this far reads as "not a serious B2B tool" against competitors buyers have already been quoted.

**Recommendation:**
- Free: keep roughly as-is (1 project/10 people/500MB) — matches the market's typical free-tier shape.
- Pro: **raise to $149–$249/month flat**, unlimited projects, a real people ceiling (e.g. 50–100, not literally unlimited) rather than truly unlimited-everything at one flat price, 10–25GB storage. Still undercuts every per-seat competitor researched by a wide margin at 10–30 people (vs. ~$240/mo ClickUp Business, ~$500/mo Asana Advanced, ~$780/mo Fieldwire Pro for 20 people) while reading as a credible business tool.
- Consider a third tier (~$399–599/mo) for larger EPC firms with 100+ people — mirrors the 3+ tier shape every competitor uses.
- Add an annual discount (~15–20%, the market norm) once the base price is set.
- Do **not** adopt ACV-based pricing — it's the most-repeated Procore complaint found.

Confidence: [Certain] on the qualitative direction (flat > per-seat, raise the number, add a people ceiling, consider a third tier); [Likely] on the specific $149–$249 anchor (reasoned from real competitor data points, not a Tielora-specific willingness-to-pay study).
