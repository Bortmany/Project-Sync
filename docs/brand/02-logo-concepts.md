# Tielora logo concepts

Ten concepts, expanded from `Agents/logo-concepts.json` (key `tielora`). Prompts are verbatim from that file; append its `style_suffix` when generating: "Flat vector logo, clean geometric shapes, solid fills only, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no texture, no mockup, no extra objects, centered with generous negative space on a plain background, professional SaaS brand identity, crisp scalable edges, legible at favicon size."

Shared rules: colours only from `src/app/globals.css` (primary #2e5aac, ink #152647, mid #1f3d77, teal #46c4b0, text #4a4e57, gray #a9aeb8, stone #d8d2c4). Never red. Wordmark in Candara or Segoe UI, medium weight, sentence case "Tielora".

---

CONCEPT 1
- Name: Tielora Wordmark
- Logo type: Wordmark
- Concept: "Tielora" in a humanist sans, sentence case, the i-dot in teal.
- Meaning: Plain, calm, engineering-grade.
- Why it relates to the product: the product's promise is plainness and truth; a wordmark with one quiet accent says the same.
- How it fits the existing brand: it is the app header already, in the app's own typeface and sentence case.
- Visual characteristics: single word, even stroke weight, generous letterspacing, one teal circle.
- Typography direction: Candara medium, tracking +2 percent, no ligatures.
- Color usage: ink #152647 letters, teal #46c4b0 dot, white ground; reverse to white on ink.
- Icon/symbol description: none; the teal i-dot doubles as favicon.
- Where it works best: app header, email footer, documents, invoices.
- Potential weaknesses: no standalone symbol; teal dot alone is weak at 16 px.
- Higgsfield prompt: Wordmark logo reading 'Tielora' in a clean humanist sans-serif (Candara style, medium weight) in ink blue #152647, the dot of the letter i drawn as a small teal #46c4b0 circle, no symbol, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 2
- Name: T-Beam
- Logo type: Lettermark
- Concept: A capital T drawn as a structural T-beam cross-section.
- Meaning: Built for engineers; load-bearing.
- Why it relates to the product: the audience builds structures; a beam says the software carries load too.
- How it fits the existing brand: blue on white, geometric, no ornament.
- Visual characteristics: heavy stem, wide flange, square corners, thin teal line on top.
- Typography direction: pair with the Concept 1 wordmark at the right.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac beam, teal #46c4b0 flange line.
- Icon/symbol description: T-beam silhouette, roughly square bounding box.
- Where it works best: favicon, app icon, hard-hat sticker.
- Potential weaknesses: literal, reads as construction only; teal line vanishes when small.
- Higgsfield prompt: Lettermark logo: a bold capital letter T drawn as a structural steel T-beam cross-section in primary blue #2e5aac with a thin teal #46c4b0 flange line along the top edge, geometric and precise, flat vector, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 3
- Name: Converge
- Logo type: Abstract symbol
- Concept: Four lines from different directions meet at one node.
- Meaning: Every company, every discipline, one source of truth.
- Why it relates to the product: multiple companies and disciplines meet at one task board; the node is the derived truth.
- How it fits the existing brand: matches "One project. Every company." and the teal highlight rule.
- Visual characteristics: four equal-weight strokes, asymmetric entry angles, one solid dot.
- Typography direction: wordmark to the right, x-height aligned to the node.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac lines, teal #46c4b0 node.
- Icon/symbol description: a star of four spokes ending in a centre circle.
- Where it works best: symbol on its own, social avatar, loading state.
- Potential weaknesses: resembles generic network marks; needs care not to look like a crosshair.
- Higgsfield prompt: Abstract logo symbol: four straight lines in primary blue #2e5aac entering from different directions and converging on one solid teal #46c4b0 circular node at the centre, clean and balanced, flat vector, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 4
- Name: Hex Node
- Logo type: Geometric symbol
- Concept: A hexagon outline with a teal centre node linked to three vertices.
- Meaning: Disciplines around one coordinated project.
- Why it relates to the product: six sides for six disciplines, one centre where status is worked out.
- How it fits the existing brand: technical and calm; ink outline, teal accent.
- Visual characteristics: thin outline, three spokes, one dot, plenty of air.
- Typography direction: wordmark below, centred, for a stacked lockup.
- Color usage: ink #152647 outline and spokes, teal #46c4b0 dot.
- Icon/symbol description: hexagon with a partial internal star.
- Where it works best: app icon, badges, document stamps.
- Potential weaknesses: hexagons are common in industrial SaaS; thin outline needs a bold variant for small sizes.
- Higgsfield prompt: Geometric logo: a regular hexagon outline in ink blue #152647 with a solid teal #46c4b0 dot at its centre connected by thin lines to three alternating vertices, technical and calm, flat vector, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 5
- Name: Bridge Gap
- Logo type: Negative-space symbol
- Concept: Two blue blocks whose gap forms a capital T bridging them.
- Meaning: The space between companies is where Tielora works.
- Why it relates to the product: the product lives in the gap between contractors; the T is that gap made useful.
- How it fits the existing brand: bold, solid, one colour; matches the plain voice.
- Visual characteristics: two rounded rectangles, a white T between them, strong silhouette.
- Typography direction: wordmark to the right, cap height equal to block height.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac blocks; single-colour mark, teal optional in the wordmark dot.
- Icon/symbol description: two blocks, negative-space T.
- Where it works best: favicon, app icon, dark-mode reverse.
- Potential weaknesses: negative space collapses if the gap is too thin; model may fill the gap.
- Higgsfield prompt: Negative-space logo: two solid rounded rectangles in primary blue #2e5aac placed side by side with a gap between them shaped like a capital letter T in the white background colour, bold silhouette, flat, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 6
- Name: Converge Lockup
- Logo type: Symbol + wordmark
- Concept: The converge node left of the Tielora wordmark.
- Meaning: Symbol and name for the app header.
- Why it relates to the product: same meaning as Concept 3 with the name attached for first-time visitors.
- How it fits the existing brand: it is the natural header of the app shell and landing page.
- Visual characteristics: small symbol, generous gap, wordmark at optical centre.
- Typography direction: Candara medium, ink; symbol height equals cap height.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac lines, teal #46c4b0 node, ink #152647 wordmark.
- Icon/symbol description: Converge mark at roughly 0.9 of cap height.
- Where it works best: app header, landing page, email header, sales deck.
- Potential weaknesses: long horizontal footprint; symbol becomes tiny in narrow layouts.
- Higgsfield prompt: Logo lockup: a small symbol of four lines converging on a teal #46c4b0 node in primary blue #2e5aac at left, then the word 'Tielora' in a clean humanist sans-serif medium in ink blue #152647, horizontal lockup, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 7
- Name: TL Monogram
- Logo type: Monogram
- Concept: T and L sharing one vertical stroke.
- Meaning: One backbone shared by two letters, like one workspace shared by companies.
- Why it relates to the product: one shared structure serving two parties is the whole idea of a multi-company project.
- How it fits the existing brand: geometric, single colour, quiet teal detail.
- Visual characteristics: T's stem is the L's stem; square foot in teal.
- Typography direction: monogram stands alone; wordmark used separately.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac letters, teal #46c4b0 square.
- Icon/symbol description: combined T and L in one shape.
- Where it works best: app icon, favicon, watermark on exported documents.
- Potential weaknesses: TL means nothing to newcomers; may read as a T with a serif.
- Higgsfield prompt: Monogram logo: capital letters T and L sharing a single vertical stroke as one geometric shape in primary blue #2e5aac, the L's foot ending in a small teal #46c4b0 square, precise, flat vector, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 8
- Name: Gantt Sign-off
- Logo type: Industry-inspired symbol
- Concept: Three staggered horizontal bars joined by one vertical sign-off line.
- Meaning: Discipline tasks rolling up into one milestone.
- Why it relates to the product: it is the stage-gated Gantt and the derived status in one glyph.
- How it fits the existing brand: it looks like the product's own timeline component.
- Visual characteristics: three rounded bars of different lengths, one vertical teal line on the right.
- Typography direction: wordmark below or to the right, bars aligned to the baseline.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac bars, teal #46c4b0 gate line.
- Icon/symbol description: mini Gantt with a milestone line.
- Where it works best: feature icons, product screenshots, favicon.
- Potential weaknesses: close to a menu or chart icon; risk of looking like a generic UI glyph.
- Higgsfield prompt: Symbol logo: three horizontal rounded bars of different lengths stacked and staggered like a Gantt chart in primary blue #2e5aac, joined on the right by one vertical teal #46c4b0 line, flat vector, minimal, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 9
- Name: Tie Knot
- Logo type: Conceptual symbol
- Concept: Two loops tied in a simple knot.
- Meaning: 'Tie' in Tielora: companies tied into one project.
- Why it relates to the product: two parties held together by one line, which is what the audit trail and the shared board do.
- How it fits the existing brand: two brand colours, single continuous line, calm.
- Visual characteristics: one continuous path, two loops, colour changes at the knot.
- Typography direction: wordmark to the right, loops sized to cap height.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac loop, teal #46c4b0 loop.
- Icon/symbol description: a flat overhand knot from two loops.
- Where it works best: social avatar, stickers, hero illustration seed.
- Potential weaknesses: knots can read as tangles or as a medical ribbon; the model may add a third loop.
- Higgsfield prompt: Conceptual logo: two smooth loops drawn as a single continuous line tied into a simple flat knot, one loop primary blue #2e5aac and the other teal #46c4b0, geometric and clean, flat vector, white background #ffffff.

CONCEPT 10
- Name: Truth Line
- Logo type: Minimal premium mark
- Concept: One horizontal blue line with a single teal dot sitting on it.
- Meaning: One source of truth.
- Why it relates to the product: one line, one point; the daily brief and derived status reduced to their essence.
- How it fits the existing brand: the most restrained option, matching the calm voice and the honesty band's teal dots.
- Visual characteristics: thick rounded rule, one dot near the right end, wide margins.
- Typography direction: wordmark sits on the line as if it were a baseline.
- Color usage: primary #2e5aac line, teal #46c4b0 dot.
- Icon/symbol description: a rule and a dot.
- Where it works best: loading bar, divider, premium print, dark reverse.
- Potential weaknesses: too abstract to stand alone; reads as a slider control.
- Higgsfield prompt: Minimal premium logo mark: one thick horizontal rounded line in primary blue #2e5aac with a single solid teal #46c4b0 circle resting on top of it near the right end, extreme simplicity, generous empty space, white background #ffffff.
