# Tielora product and brand review

PRODUCT
A web app for coordinating engineering projects across every company involved. Self-serve signup with an industry template (Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Civil, Process, HSE). Main tasks split into discipline tasks. Status is derived from the work, never typed in: a task can never say done unless its work is done. Documents are versioned and never overwritten, required documents gate completion, every action goes to a permanent audit trail, and a stage-gated Gantt shows the project. A computed daily brief is copied to Slack or Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive connect, and external contractors get limited accounts scoped per project. Free and Pro have every feature; Pro only adds room. Paddle billing. English, desktop-first.

TARGET CUSTOMER
Engineering companies in oil and gas, energy and construction, Gulf and international, running projects with several contractors.

PRIMARY USER
Project managers and discipline leads, 30 to 55, who own the schedule but not the people doing the work, drowning in email chains and spreadsheets.

INDUSTRY
Engineering project delivery: EPC, plant, construction.

CORE PROBLEM
Each company keeps its own tracker, so the project has several truths. Typed statuses drift, documents get overwritten, contractors get everything or nothing, and every morning starts with finding out what changed.

CORE VALUE PROPOSITION
One project. Every company. One source of truth. Derived, not typed in. Nothing gets overwritten. Outside help, safely scoped. One page, every morning.

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS
- Derived status with required-document gating; even an admin cannot override it.
- Versioned documents and an audit trail nobody can edit or delete.
- Contractor accounts scoped to one project and their tasks only, with a sign-off queue.
- Computed daily brief posted to the channel the team already uses.
- Every feature on both plans; templates seed disciplines at signup.

EXISTING BRAND PERSONALITY
Calm and precise. Plain English, sentence case, no jargon, no exclamation marks. Says only what is true: no testimonials or numbers until they exist. Dates like 30 Sep 2026.
