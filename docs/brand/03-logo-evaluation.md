# Tielora logo evaluation

Scores all ten concepts from `02-logo-concepts.md` against the ten criteria in the brief. Each criterion is scored 1 (weak) to 5 (strong). The scores are the design team's reading of the concept descriptions and the Higgsfield renders; the final choice below was made by the owner, not by the totals.

Criteria in plain words: brand relevance (does it say "many companies, one source of truth", calmly), memorability (would you recognise it a week later), simplicity (how few shapes), scalability (16 px to a site hoarding without redrawing), distinctiveness (does it stand apart from other construction and project software), SaaS suitability (does it sit well in the app shell, a sales deck and an email header), favicon (16 px in a browser tab), app icon (512 px rounded square), advertisement (does it carry an ad or a LinkedIn post), long-term usability (does it still fit after five years and new modules).

## Tradeoffs

The Converge mark (3) leads because it is the only symbol that says what the product does without borrowing from the construction trade: several parties arriving at one point is exactly "one project, every company", and the teal node is the derived truth the app computes. Its risks are that four-spoke marks are a familiar network shape and, if drawn symmetrically, a crosshair; both are handled by giving the spokes uneven angles and a node clearly larger than the stroke. The Wordmark (1) is the most honest to the brand voice (plain, sentence case, one quiet accent) and is already the app header, but a word cannot be a favicon, so it needs a symbol beside it. The Converge Lockup (6) is that pairing and is the strongest header and ad asset of the ten. The TL Monogram (7) is the best pure favicon and app icon, a tight shape with a clean silhouette, but "TL" means nothing to a first-time visitor and can read as a T with a serif. The Hex Node (4) has good meaning (six disciplines around one centre) and a solid app-icon presence, but hexagons are the default shape of industrial software and its thin outline needs a bold redraw for small sizes. The T-Beam (2) and Bridge Gap (5) are strong silhouettes but literal or letter-bound; Gantt Sign-off (8) and Truth Line (10) look like UI controls; the Tie Knot (9) is the most distinctive and the least usable, since knots read as tangles at small sizes.

## Selected for refinement

These four were the owner's selections, made after viewing the ten Higgsfield renders in the gallery. Two of them (the Converge Lockup and the TL Monogram) sit in the top three by total; the Wordmark (35) was chosen because it is the brand voice itself and pairs with any symbol, and the Hex Node (32) was chosen over higher-scoring options because the owner wanted a stronger geometric app-icon candidate in the mix. The owner's picks are the ones that go to refinement.

1. **Concept 1, Tielora Wordmark.** The name in Candara or Segoe UI, medium weight, sentence case, with a teal i-dot. It is the header already, and it is the wordmark half of every lockup below.
2. **Concept 6, Converge Lockup.** The Converge symbol beside the wordmark. The natural header, email header and deck asset. It is refined here as the primary logo.
3. **Concept 7, TL Monogram.** T and L sharing one stem, teal foot. Kept as the small-size and watermark candidate.
4. **Concept 4, Hex Node.** A hexagon with a teal centre node linked to three vertices. Kept as an app-icon and badge candidate, redrawn with a heavier outline.

Not carried forward: 2, 5 (letter-bound silhouettes), 3 on its own (it is carried inside 6), 8, 9, 10.
