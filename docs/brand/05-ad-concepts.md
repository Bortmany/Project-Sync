# Tielora ad concepts

Twenty concepts, four groups of five, one psychological angle each. Every claim maps to a built feature described in `src/app/(public)/page.tsx` and `src/app/(public)/pricing/page.tsx`. No testimonials, no customer names, no usage numbers: Tielora has none to cite yet and the landing page says nothing that is not true.

Every concept ends with the same two CTAs: "Start free, no card" and "See the demo company".

## PERFORMANCE

1. The Thursday status
- Type: performance, static or 15 s motion
- Angle: pain-point
- Hook: "Someone typed done on Thursday. The civil pack arrived on the 30 Sep 2026."
- Visual: a spreadsheet row reading done, slowly greyed out; under it the real Tielora parent task showing blocked, on a laptop.
- CTA: Start free, no card / See the demo company

2. Three trackers, one project
- Type: performance, carousel or 15 s
- Angle: problem to solution
- Hook: "Three contractors. Three trackers. One project that only exists in your inbox."
- Visual: three overlapping spreadsheet windows collapse into one Tielora task board on a laptop.
- CTA: Start free, no card / See the demo company

3. Before ten, after ten
- Type: performance, split-frame static
- Angle: before and after
- Hook: "Before: 40 emails to find out what changed. After: one page."
- Visual: left half an inbox at 07:12, right half the real daily brief on the same laptop at 07:13.
- CTA: Start free, no card / See the demo company

4. Spreadsheet or Tielora
- Type: performance, table static
- Angle: comparison
- Hook: "A spreadsheet lets you type done. Tielora does not."
- Visual: a plain two-column table: typed status vs derived status, overwritten files vs versioned files, everyone sees everything vs contractors see their tasks.
- CTA: Start free, no card / See the demo company

5. Stop typing statuses
- Type: performance, 30 s
- Angle: you are doing this wrong
- Hook: "This is where projects go wrong."
- Visual: macro of fingers typing d o n e into a cell, then the real Tielora task that will not complete while a required document is missing.
- CTA: Start free, no card / See the demo company

## SELECTED FOR PRODUCTION

- Concept A: 6, Nobody typed that status (UGC, PM on camera in a site office). The strongest pain in the audience's own words, and the derived-status idea lands in one sentence. Handheld and human, so it reads native in a LinkedIn feed.
- Concept B: 16, One page, every morning (product led, laptop hero). The daily brief and task board are the most photogenic true features. Desktop-first, so the laptop is the frame and real screenshots carry the proof.
- Concept C: 11, The gap between companies (cinematic). Says the brand line "One project. Every company." in pictures. Also crops to a website hero and sales deck opener.
- Concept D: 5, Stop typing statuses (performance). Cheapest to produce, works with sound off, and the "you are doing this wrong" angle drives clicks without any claim we cannot back.
- Concept E: 17, Outside help, safely scoped (product demo). The contractor account is the differentiator competitors do not show; a demo proves it faster than words.

Full production packages for A to E are in `docs/brand/06-production-ads.md`.
